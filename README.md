# 🛒 E-commerce Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **E-commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes e-commerce sales performance, product categories, geographical sales distribution, yearly sales trends, order quantities, and product returns. It provides interactive visualizations to help understand customer purchasing patterns, identify top-performing products, and evaluate overall business performance.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze total sales across different locations.
* Compare yearly sales performance.
* Analyze sales by product sub-category.
* Identify the product category with the highest returns.
* Compare delivered and returned order quantities.
* Monitor sales performance across product categories.
* Explore product-level sales details.
* Provide interactive filtering by product category, zone, and year.
* Present meaningful business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains e-commerce sales records with information about products, categories, geographical zones, sales, order quantities, and order status.

Key fields used in the analysis include:

* `Product_Name` – Name of the product.
* `Product_Category` – Category to which the product belongs.
* `Sub_Category` – Specific product sub-category.
* `Zone` – Geographical zone associated with the sale.
* `Year` – Year of the transaction.
* `Sales` – Sales amount generated by the product.
* `Order_Quantity` – Quantity of products ordered.
* `Status` – Order status, such as Delivered or Returned.
* `Location` – Geographical location associated with sales.

*Note: These field names are based on the visible dashboard labels and assumed underlying data structure. Adjust them to match the actual dataset.*
